# Sell from home on Sellio, for free

Sellio gives home-based sellers in Singapore, Malaysia and Indonesia a free shop: your own
page to share, a place on Sellio Explore where new buyers find you, and orders that come
straight to your phone.

It suits home bakers and cooks, crafters, and small brands that sell from home.

## What you get for free

- **Your own shop page** at `sellio.sg/store/<your-shop>`, with your logo and colours.
- **Up to 10 listings** and **100 orders a month**. No card needed.
- **Get paid directly.** Buyers pay you with your own payment QR: PayNow in Singapore, DuitNow
  in Malaysia, QRIS in Indonesia. The money goes straight to you.
- **Self-collection, delivery, or both.** Set your delivery fee, and an amount above which
  delivery is free.
- **Buyers find you.** Your listings appear on Sellio Explore under **Home sellers**, and
  your shop page and listings are in Sellio's sitemap for Google.
- **Chat.** Buyers can message you about a listing before they order.
- **Star ratings** from your buyers, shown on your listings.

## Set up your shop in about 5 minutes

1. **Create an account** at [sellio.sg](https://sellio.sg) or in the
   [Android app](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.base6985959ce9aaceadb8b7cc41.app)
   with your phone number, email or Google.
2. **Choose "Sell from home"** (marked Free). Already browsing Explore? Tap **Me → Start
   selling**.
3. **Your shop's look:** add a logo and pick your colours.
4. **How you sell:** self-collection, delivery, or both.
5. **Get paid:** upload your PayNow, DuitNow or QRIS code. Buyers see it after they order.
6. **Your first listings:** a photo, a name, a price and a short description for each. You can
   add up to 10 on the free plan.
7. **Share your shop:** send your shop link to your WhatsApp groups, friends and regular
   buyers.

You can skip any step and change it later in **Settings**. Until you finish, your Dashboard
shows **Finish setting up**.

## When an order comes in

1. The order waits for you in **Orders** until you accept it.
2. Once you accept, the buyer sees your collection point (for self-collection). Your exact
   address stays hidden until then.
3. The buyer pays you with your payment QR. Check the payment came in before you hand the
   order over.
4. Prepare the order and hand it over or deliver it.

In the Android app, allow notifications when Sellio asks, so new orders reach you even when
Sellio isn't open.

## Get more buyers without spending on ads

- **Use good photos.** Bright, close-up photos sell; you can add more than one photo to a
  listing.
- **Name things the way buyers search.** "Pandan chiffon cake (8 inch)" is found more often
  than "Cake A".
- **Share your link everywhere you already are:** WhatsApp status, Telegram groups, Facebook,
  Instagram bio.
- **Answer chats quickly.** Buyers often ask before they order.
- **Ask happy buyers to rate you.** Ratings show on your listings and help new buyers trust you.
- **Stay on Explore.** Your shop is listed there by default. You can switch it off in
  **Settings** if you only want to sell to people you send your link to.

logins? Move up to a business plan from your shop. Your first business plan starts with a
**7-day free trial**, and your shop keeps its link, listings and orders. See
