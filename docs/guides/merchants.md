# Run your business on Sellio

Sellio gives cafés, restaurants, hawker and kiosk businesses, retail shops and service
businesses in Singapore, Malaysia and Indonesia one place for every order: at the counter, at
the table and online. It also puts your shop in front of people who have never heard of you.

## What you get

**Take orders everywhere**

- **POS counter** for walk-in orders, paid by cash, card or your payment QR (PayNow, DuitNow or
  QRIS).
- **QR ordering at your tables.** Customers scan, order and send it to your kitchen. No app or
  account needed on the web.
- **Your own online storefront** at `sellio.sg/store/<your-shop>`, in your colours, with your
  logo and photos, in a grid, list or split layout.
- **Dine-in and takeaway**, ordered at the counter, at the table or from your storefront.
- **Opening hours.** Outside them your storefront shows you're closed.

**Run the kitchen and the shop**

- **Orders and a kitchen display** that update live, with a sound for each new order.
- **Kitchen chits and receipts** on Bluetooth thermal printers.
- **A table bill:** a table pays once for everything it ordered.
- **Staff logins with roles** (owner, manager, cashier, kitchen and more), so everyone sees only
  what they need.
- **Inventory and reports.**
- **Sellio AI tools:** turn a photo of your menu into products, and get help with product
  photos and descriptions.
- **Push alerts** for new orders in the Android app.

## Get seen without a marketing plan

Most small businesses don't have time or money for ads. Sellio brings customers to you:

- **Sellio Explore.** Anyone can browse Sellio's shops and items, without an account. Your shop
  is listed by default, under **Merchants**, and food businesses also show under **Food &
  drinks**.
- **Google.** Your storefront and items are in Sellio's sitemap, so search engines can find and
  list them.
- **Ratings.** Customers rate your shop from 1 to 5 stars after ordering. You can hide the stars
  on your own ordering page if you prefer; they still show on Explore.
- **Your link and QR codes everywhere.** Put your table QR codes on tables, and your storefront
  link on WhatsApp, Instagram, Google Maps and your packaging.
- **Sellio World (coming soon).** An island map of Sellio's shops, with districts for F&B,
  retail and services. F&B opens first.

## Get started

1. **Choose a plan** at [sellio.sg](https://sellio.sg). New businesses start with a **7-day free
   trial**.
2. **Set up your business:** your name and logo, your menu or products, opening hours and
   tables. Have a printed menu? Scan it and Sellio adds the items for you.
3. **Design your storefront:** colours, layout, banner and photos.
4. **Print your table QR codes** from **Tables & QR**.
5. **Invite your staff** from **User Management** and give each a role.
6. **Connect your printer** in **Settings**.
7. **Share your storefront link**, and you're open.

Already selling from home on Sellio's free plan? Upgrade from your shop: you keep your link,
listings and orders.
