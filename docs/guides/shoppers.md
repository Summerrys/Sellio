# Shop on Sellio

Sellio Explore brings together home-based sellers and local businesses in Singapore, Malaysia
and Indonesia: home-baked cakes, meals, crafts, cafés and shops, all in one place.

## Browse, no account needed

- Open [sellio.sg/explore](https://sellio.sg/explore) or the
  [Android app](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.base6985959ce9aaceadb8b7cc41.app).
- Filter by **Food & drinks**, **Goods**, **Home sellers** or **Merchants**, or search for what
  you want.
- Tap an item for its photos, price and shop, or open the shop's page to see everything it
  sells.

## Order

1. Add items to your cart. Each shop has its own cart, and you check out on that shop's page.
2. Choose how you get it: self-collection or delivery from home sellers, dine-in or takeaway
   from businesses.
3. Pay the shop the way its checkout shows, usually with its PayNow, DuitNow or QRIS code.
   Your payment goes straight to the shop.
4. The shop accepts your order. For self-collection from a home seller, you see the exact
   collection address once they accept.

On the website and at a shop's tables you can order without an account. In the Sellio app,
you sign in before placing an order.

**At a café or restaurant table:** scan the QR code on your table, order, and it goes straight
to the kitchen.

## With a free account

- **My purchases:** everything you've ordered, from every shop, and where each order is.
- **Chat** with a seller about a listing before you buy.
- **Like** listings to find them again later.
- **Rate** the shop from 1 to 5 stars after an order. You can change your rating for 30 days.

## Want to sell too?

Anyone can open a free shop. Tap **Me → Start selling**, or see
[Sell from home on Sellio, for free](home-sellers.md).
